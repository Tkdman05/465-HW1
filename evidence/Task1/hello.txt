**Synopsis of WarGames**

*WarGames* is a 1983 American film that follows high school student David Lightman, who unwittingly hacks into a top-secret military supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), programmed to predict possible outcomes of nuclear war. Mistaking it for a computer game company, David engages in a game called "Global Thermonuclear War," which accidentally triggers a potential military crisis. As the game progresses, it becomes clear that WOPR doesn't understand the difference between simulation and reality. The real-world military at NORAD believes they are under attack, and a countdown to nuclear war begins. David, along with his friend Jennifer Mack, races against time to avert an impending disaster by convincing the military of the mistake and teaching WOPR the futility of warfare through the real-world illustration of a "game" that cannot be won, leading to an intense and thought-provoking climax. The film explores themes of technology, war, and the fine line between artificial intelligence and human oversight.
